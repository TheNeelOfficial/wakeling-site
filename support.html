The alarm did not ring

This is the one that matters, so please do report it. The usual cause is the app being swiped out of the app switcher, which stops it running — leave Wakeling open in the background overnight. Also check notification permission, and that Focus or Do Not Disturb is not silencing it.

Testing your alarm

Settings → Alarm → Test the alarm rings a full alarm in ten seconds, so you can check it works without waiting until morning.

Cancelling Pro

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: Settings app → your name → Subscriptions. Cancelling takes effect at the end of the paid period. The free trial needs no card and there is nothing to cancel.

Turning off analytics

Settings → Privacy → Analytics turns off crash reports and usage counts together.
